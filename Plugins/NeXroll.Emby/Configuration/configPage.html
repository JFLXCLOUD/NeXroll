NeXroll Intros

This plugin is automatically configured by your NeXroll server. Preroll intros are injected before movies and episodes based on your NeXroll schedules.

Playback Options

Only used with NeXroll 2.2.0 and older, where 0 means 1. From NeXroll 2.2.1, NeXroll decides what plays: one preroll from a random category (the number is set in NeXroll, on the Connect page) and every block of a sequence, so this setting is ignored.
